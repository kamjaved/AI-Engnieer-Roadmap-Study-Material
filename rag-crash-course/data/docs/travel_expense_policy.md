All domestic business travel above 200 km one-way requires manager approval submitted through the travel request form at least 5 working days before travel, except client-emergency visits, which may be approved retroactively by the VP of Sales within 48 hours. International travel additionally requires COO sign-off regardless of grade.

Per diem rates for domestic travel are ₹1,500/day for staff and executives, ₹2,000/day for managers, and ₹2,800/day for GM level and above, covering meals and incidentals; accommodation and travel fare are booked and billed separately through the company's designated travel desk, not reimbursed as personal expense. Employees booking travel independently, outside the travel desk, will only be reimbursed up to the fare the travel desk would have charged for an equivalent booking, with the difference borne personally.

International per diem is set per destination by Finance at the time of travel approval, since rates vary significantly by country and are reviewed quarterly against currency fluctuation.

Expense reports must be submitted within 7 working days of returning from travel, via the expense portal, with original receipts attached for any single expense above ₹500. Expenses submitted more than 30 days after travel completion require CFO approval regardless of amount, and repeated late submissions may result in a temporary suspension of travel-desk booking privileges for that employee.

Local travel (within city, for client visits or vendor site inspections) is reimbursed at ₹8/km for two-wheelers and ₹12/km for four-wheelers, based on odometer readings logged in the expense portal, or actual auto/cab fare with receipt.

Non-reimbursable expenses include alcohol (except at client-facing business dinners with prior manager approval), personal entertainment, traffic fines, and travel companion costs for a non-employee. Client entertainment expenses above ₹5,000 per event require the client's company name and business purpose to be recorded in the expense portal, not just the amount.
