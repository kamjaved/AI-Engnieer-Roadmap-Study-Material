# Turab Industries — Product Catalog & Corporate Services Guide

## Introduction

Turab Industries partners with enterprise clients across IT services, banking, hospitality, and manufacturing to design, produce, and deliver branded apparel, promotional merchandise, and corporate gifting programs at scale. This guide walks through our four product lines and the services that support them, for procurement and HR teams evaluating us as a supply partner.

## Corporate Apparel & Uniforms

Our apparel division produces formal wear, branded polos, outerwear, and role-specific workwear for security, hospitality, and retail staff. Fabric options range from standard cotton-polyester blends to premium 100% cotton pique for client-facing roles, and moisture-wicking synthetic blends for outdoor or high-activity uniform requirements. All uniform fabric is available in OEKO-TEX Standard 100 certified variants at a modest premium, increasingly requested by clients with their own sustainability disclosure requirements. Typical use cases include annual uniform refresh cycles for facility-management contracts, and one-time branded apparel runs for company offsites or anniversaries.

## Promotional Merchandise

Our merchandise line covers pens, diaries, drinkware, bags, and desk accessories, sourced and branded for trade shows, client-facing events, and internal recognition programs. Enterprise clients most commonly order this line ahead of annual conferences or as part of a recurring quarterly gifting cadence for top clients. We maintain minimum stock of blank merchandise for the top 20 SKUs specifically to support short-notice event orders, though this does not override the standard MOQ or lead-time policy for a first-time order.

## Employee Welcome Kits

Welcome kits are assembled by combining items from our apparel and merchandise lines into a single onboarding package, optionally with a printed welcome letter or personalized name card. Enterprise HR teams typically standardize on one kit tier company-wide, though we support mixed-tier orders (for example, a Premium Kit for leadership hires and a Standard Kit for other new joiners within the same PO). Kit assembly happens at our Tirupur facility, and kits ship pre-packed and ready for HR to hand over on an employee's first day, rather than requiring on-site assembly by the client.

## Corporate Gifting

Our gifting line covers festive hampers and executive-tier gift sets for both client-facing gifting (relationship management, key account appreciation) and internal employee gifting (festivals, service milestones). Hampers can combine third-party sourced food and lifestyle items with our own branded merchandise, and we offer individually addressed, pan-India dispatch for large distributed gifting programs rather than requiring bulk delivery to a single client address.

## Corporate Services

Beyond manufacturing, we offer three services to enterprise clients. Design Consultation: our in-house design team develops branding mockups and material recommendations before a client commits to a sample run, at no charge for orders above 250 units. Dedicated Account Management: clients on an annual supply contract are assigned a single point of contact for reordering, production tracking, and issue resolution, rather than routing through general sales. Pan-India Logistics: we manage last-mile delivery to distributed office locations or individual employee addresses (for remote-hire welcome kits), coordinated through our logistics partners with tracking shared directly to the client's HR or procurement contact.

## Why Enterprise Clients Choose Us

Consistent quality across bulk reorders, in-house OEKO-TEX certified fabric sourcing, and a single manufacturing site that keeps production timelines predictable are the reasons most-cited by clients renewing annual contracts. We are positioned for clients who value a long-term, contract-based supply relationship over one-off transactional ordering.
