All Turab Industries employees follow a five-and-a-half-day work week: Monday to Friday, 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, and a half day on Saturday, 9:30 AM to 1:30 PM, for manufacturing and corporate staff alike. Core hours (10:30 AM–4:30 PM) require in-person presence for corporate office staff; manufacturing floor staff follow fixed shift timings with no flexibility, since production lines run on coordinated shift handovers.

New hires undergo a 90-day probation period, extendable once by 30 days at a manager's discretion if performance is inconclusive rather than clearly unsatisfactory. Confirmation requires a written sign-off from the reporting manager and HR. Background verification (address, prior employment, and educational credentials) is completed within the first 30 days for all corporate hires; manufacturing floor hires require only address and identity verification.

Notice period for resignation is 30 days for staff level and below, and 60 days for managers and above, counted from the date of written resignation acceptance, not the date of submission. Buyout of notice period is permitted only with COO approval and is capped at 30 days' equivalent salary.

Corporate office staff follow a business-casual dress code; manufacturing floor staff are issued two sets of company uniforms at joining (a benefit of working at an apparel manufacturer, replaced annually or on visible wear, whichever comes first) and are required to wear them on shift for safety and brand-consistency reasons.

Turab Industries maintains a zero-tolerance anti-harassment policy. Complaints are handled by the Internal Committee (IC) constituted under the POSH Act, with a mandatory acknowledgment within 3 working days and resolution targeted within 90 days. Disciplinary action for policy violations follows a documented three-stage process: verbal warning, written warning, and termination, except in cases of gross misconduct (theft, harassment, safety violations), which may bypass earlier stages entirely.
