Turab Industries manufactures four product categories for enterprise clients: Corporate Apparel & Uniforms (formal shirts, polos, blazers, role-specific workwear for security/hospitality/retail staff), Promotional Merchandise (pens, diaries, drinkware, laptop bags, desk organizers), Employee Welcome Kits (curated onboarding boxes combining apparel and merchandise), and Corporate Gifts (festive hampers and premium gift sets for client or employee gifting occasions).

Minimum order quantity (MOQ) is 100 units per SKU for apparel and uniforms, 250 units per SKU for promotional merchandise, and 50 kits for welcome kit or corporate gift orders, since kit assembly has higher per-unit handling cost than single-product runs. Orders below MOQ are accepted only at a 25% small-batch surcharge, subject to production slot availability.

Customization options include embroidery (up to 4 colors, included in base pricing above 200 units), screen printing (up to 6 colors), and heat-transfer branding for items unsuitable for embroidery (e.g. drinkware, bags). A physical sample is provided for approval before any bulk run above 500 units; sample turnaround is 5–7 working days and sample cost is adjusted against the final order if the client proceeds within 60 days.

Standard lead time from purchase order and approved sample to delivery is 3–4 weeks for apparel and merchandise, and 2–3 weeks for welcome kits and gift hampers assembled from in-stock components. Lead times extend by 1–2 weeks during the September–November festive gifting season due to production backlog across all divisions.

Payment terms for new corporate clients are 50% advance with PO confirmation and 50% before dispatch; clients with an active annual supply contract and at least 12 months' order history may be extended Net 30 terms, subject to Finance approval. Defective units identified within 15 days of delivery are replaced free of cost; claims after 15 days are assessed case-by-case and are not guaranteed. Shipping is arranged pan-India through Turab's logistics partners, with delivery tracking shared via email once dispatched.
