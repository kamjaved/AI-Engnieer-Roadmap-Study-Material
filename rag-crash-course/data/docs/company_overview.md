Turab Industries Pvt. Ltd. was founded in 2011 by Farhan Turab in Tirupur, Tamil Nadu, one of India's largest knitwear and garment manufacturing hubs. What began as a small contract-stitching unit supplying blank T-shirts to local exporters has grown into a full-service B2B manufacturer of corporate apparel, uniforms, promotional merchandise, and employee welcome kits for enterprise customers across India and the Middle East.

The company operates two facilities: a 40,000 sq. ft. manufacturing plant in Tirupur handling cutting, stitching, embroidery, and screen printing, and a corporate office in Bengaluru that houses sales, design, and key account management. Combined headcount is approximately 450 employees, with roughly 340 in manufacturing and 110 in corporate functions.

Turab Industries is organized into three business divisions. The Corporate Apparel & Uniforms division produces formal wear, branded polos, outerwear, and role-specific workwear (security, hospitality, retail) for enterprise clients. The Promotional Merchandise division manufactures branded pens, drinkware, bags, and desk accessories used for events, trade shows, and client gifting. The Welcome Kits & Corporate Gifting division assembles curated onboarding kits and festive gift hampers, combining products from the other two divisions with third-party sourced items.

Clients are primarily mid-size to large enterprises in IT services, banking, hospitality, and manufacturing, engaged on annual supply contracts rather than one-off orders. The company's stated values are on-time delivery, consistent quality across bulk orders, and design flexibility for corporate branding requirements.

Turab Industries is led by Founder & Managing Director Farhan Turab, supported by a Chief Operating Officer overseeing manufacturing and a VP of Sales overseeing the Bengaluru office. The company became ISO 9001:2015 certified in 2019 and holds OEKO-TEX Standard 100 certification for its textile inputs, a requirement increasingly asked for by enterprise procurement teams during vendor evaluation.
