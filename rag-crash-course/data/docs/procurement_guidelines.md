All raw material and vendor procurement at Turab Industries follows a tiered approval structure based on purchase order (PO) value. POs under ₹50,000 require only department-head approval. POs between ₹50,000 and ₹5,00,000 require department-head plus Finance approval. POs above ₹5,00,000 require COO sign-off in addition to Finance, and must include at least two competitive quotations unless sourced from a pre-approved sole vendor for specialty materials (e.g. a specific embroidery thread grade unavailable from alternate suppliers).

New vendors must complete an empanelment process before receiving any PO: submission of GST registration, a sample batch for quality inspection by the QC team, and — for fabric and textile suppliers specifically — OEKO-TEX Standard 100 or equivalent certification confirming the material is free of harmful substances, since several enterprise clients require this certification to be traceable through Turab's own supply chain during their vendor audits.

Fabric and trim sourcing (cotton, polyester blends, zippers, buttons, embroidery thread) is procured primarily from a list of 14 pre-approved vendors maintained by the Procurement team, reviewed annually. Sourcing from outside this list requires written justification (cost, lead time, or a client-specified material not available from listed vendors) and Procurement Head approval.

Standard payment terms with vendors are Net 45 from the date of material receipt and QC clearance, not from invoice date — an invoice submitted before QC clearance does not start the payment clock. Advance payment to a vendor (up to 30% of PO value) is permitted only for new, unlisted vendors on their first order, as a risk-mitigation measure, and requires COO approval regardless of PO size.

Turab Industries' sustainability guideline requires that at least 20% of fabric procurement value each fiscal year come from certified organic cotton or recycled polyester sources, a target introduced in 2023 and tracked quarterly by the Procurement Head against actual sourcing data, not self-reported vendor claims.
